CUBESAT
what is a cubesat? well, its basically a small satellite. sizes are standardized, 1U being the smallest (10x10x10cm) and 12U being the largest (20x20x34.05cm). This type of satellite is generally used by colleges and other teams as it is easier and cheaper than making a full custom satellite that is very large. 


TLDR ON WHAT THIS PROJECT IS
basically its a satellite that goes into a 45º inclination orbit (just LEO), measures radiation and temperature, sends that data back down to the ground station, and helps validate pre-flight simulations on shielding. I will also be testing MEMS IMUs to see how they do in space. pretty simple, I think. I am custom making the OBC and EPS in terms of electronics. I will be using a commercial ADCS. Everything else about the CubeSat is pretty much custom. It will be designed based off the SpaceX Falcon User Guide.
